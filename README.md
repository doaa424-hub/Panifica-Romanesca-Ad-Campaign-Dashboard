##  Panifica Românească – Ad Campaign Performance Dashboard

###  Project Overview

This project analyzes **localized ad campaign performance** for *Panifica Românească*, focusing on measuring **online order behavior** across different neighborhoods.
The goal is to identify high-performing channels, optimize ad spend, and improve ROI across campaigns.

---

###  Objectives

* Monitor **marketing campaign effectiveness** across locations.
* Evaluate **conversion rates** and **revenue performance**.
* Understand **customer behavior** and order trends by neighborhood.
* Provide **data-driven recommendations** for future campaigns.

---

###  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                       | Value       | Description                     |
| ------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Total Ad Spend**        | $157,317.14 | Total advertising budget used.  |
| **Total Conversions**     | 9,806       | Number of successful orders.    |
| **Total Revenue**         | $523,420.01 | Total sales generated.          |
| **ROI (ROAS)**            | 4.95        | Revenue ÷ Ad Spend.             |
| **Cancellation Rate**     | —           | (Cancelled / Total Bookings).   |
| **Average Stylist Score** | —           | Service quality average rating. |

---

###  Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, transformation, and dashboard building.
* **Power Query** – Data preparation and metrics calculation.
* **Pivot Tables & Charts** – Visualization and interactivity.
* **PowerPoint / PDF** – Reporting and presentation design.

---

###  Dashboard Features

* Interactive slicers by **Neighborhood**, **Service Type**, **Channel**, and **Employee**.
* KPI cards for instant campaign insight.
* Trend charts showing monthly performance.
* Breakdown of **revenue per time of day** and **marketing channel**.
* Insights into **customer type (new vs returning)**.

---

###  Key Insights

* **Morning** and **Night** ads deliver the highest revenue impact.
* **Returning customers** contribute the largest share of income.
* **Google Ads** and **Facebook** outperform other channels in ROI.
* Revenue trend shows **steady monthly growth**, signaling campaign efficiency.

---

###  Recommendations

1. Reallocate ad spend to **top-performing channels (Google & Facebook)**.
2. Run **A/B testing** for low-performing areas.
3. Launch **loyalty programs** to retain high-value returning customers.
4. Schedule **ads in Morning/Night slots** for higher ROI.
5. Share **best practices** from top-performing employees.
